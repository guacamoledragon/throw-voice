# Does Discord have a native voice recorder?

No. Discord can't record a whole voice channel by itself.

What Discord has is [Clips](https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/16861982215703). Clips saves the last 30 seconds, 60 seconds, or 2 minutes of a stream when you press a hotkey. It's good for grabbing a funny moment. It won't record a meeting, a game night, or a podcast from start to finish.

## Recording the full session

For that you need a bot. With pawa:

1. [Invite pawa](https://discord.com/oauth2/authorize?client_id=338897906524225538&scope=applications.commands+bot&permissions=2251344896) to your server.
2. Join the voice channel and type `/record`.
3. Type `/save` when you're done.

You get one MP3 of the whole session, up to about four and a half hours. The full walkthrough is in [How to Record a Discord Voice Channel](record-voice-channel.md).

If you need each person on their own track, Craig does that. See [Craig vs pawa](craig-vs-pawa.md).
