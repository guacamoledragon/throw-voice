# Support

**pawa** is a Discord bot that records voice channels. **Pawa Companion** is its iOS app: pawa's
download links expire after 24 hours, and the app saves a recording to your phone, plays it with
a real transport, and transcribes it on-device. The app opens recordings from a pawa link — on
its own it has nothing to play.

## Getting help

Ask in the [pawa Discord server](https://discord.gg/gkvsNw8). That is the fastest route and the
one to use for bug reports. If you would rather not use Discord, email <jose@gdragon.tech>.

New to the bot? Start with the [quickstart](quickstart.md) and the
[command list](commands.md).

## The bot

**My recording was deleted.**
`/stop` throws the recording away. Use `/save` to keep it.

**The download link stopped working.**
Links expire 24 hours after the recording is made, and the audio is deleted with them. Download
it, or save it with Pawa Companion, while the link is fresh.

## The app

**Nothing happens when I open the app.**
Pawa Companion opens one specific recording. Tap a pawa recording link and it opens there —
launched on its own, it has nothing to play.

**Transcription says it is unavailable.**
On-device transcription needs an iPhone with Apple Intelligence, running iOS 26 or later. iOS 26
alone is not enough. Saving and playback still work without it.

**The recording will not load.**
The link has most likely expired — see above. Once it has, the app cannot get the audio back.

**Where do saved recordings go?**
Into the app's own storage on your device, reachable through the Files app. Deleting the app
deletes them.
