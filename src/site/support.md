# Pawa Companion — Support

**Pawa Companion** is the iOS app for pawa, the Discord voice-channel recording bot. pawa's
recordings expire 24 hours after they are made; this app saves one to your phone, plays it with
a real transport, and transcribes it on-device.

## Getting help

Ask in the [pawa Discord server](https://discord.gg/gkvsNw8). That is the fastest route and the
one to use for bug reports. If you would rather not use Discord, email <jose@gdragon.tech>.

## Common questions

**Nothing happens when I open the app.**
Pawa Companion opens one specific recording. Tap a pawa recording link and it opens there —
launched on its own, it has nothing to play.

**Transcription says it is unavailable.**
On-device transcription needs an iPhone with Apple Intelligence, running iOS 26 or later. iOS 26
alone is not enough. Saving and playback still work without it.

**The recording will not load.**
pawa's links are signed and expire. Once a link has expired the audio is gone from Discord's
side and the app cannot get it back. Save a recording while the link is still fresh.

**Where do saved recordings go?**
Into the app's own storage on your device, reachable through the Files app. Deleting the app
deletes them.

## Privacy and terms

See the [privacy policy](privacy.md) and [terms of use](terms.md). Short version: the app
collects nothing, and your audio and transcripts never leave your phone except to wherever you
send them from the share sheet.
