## Subscription

**What you get.** Pawa Starter is a paid plan for one Discord server. While the server is
subscribed, we keep the recordings we store for it for 30 days instead of 1 day (24 hours). The
30 days start when the recording is made, not when you buy. Recordings of that server that we
still have when you subscribe get the 30 days too, usually within an hour.

**What you do not get.** Nothing else changes. A recording the bot uploads to Discord stays
under Discord's rules, not ours.

**Who can buy.** You buy on the web, at
[app.pawa.im/v1/billing](https://app.pawa.im/v1/billing), signed in with Discord. Nothing is
sold in Pawa Companion. You must own the server, or have the Manage Server or Administrator
permission in it, and the bot must be in the server. One subscription covers one server. A
Discord account can have one subscription, and a server can have one subscription.

You must be at least 13. If you are not yet an adult where you live, a parent or guardian must
agree to the purchase.

**Moving it.** You cannot move a subscription to a different server yourself. Ask in the
[pawa Discord server](https://discord.gg/gkvsNw8) and we can move it for you.

**Price and renewal.** The price is the one the billing page shows when you buy. As of
1 October 2026 that is USD 5.00 a month. Tax is not included, and any tax that applies is
added. The subscription renews every month, automatically, and you are charged each time, until
you cancel.

**Price changes.** The price can change. A new price applies from your next renewal, not to a
period you already paid for.

**Cancelling.** On the billing page, "Manage subscription" opens the customer portal. You can
cancel there, or change your payment method. After you cancel, the subscription stays active
until the end of the period you paid for, and you are not charged again.

**If the bot leaves.** The subscription continues, and so do the charges, if the bot is no
longer in the server or the server no longer exists. You have to cancel.

**Failed payments.** If a renewal payment fails, the subscription stays active for a grace
period while the payment provider tries again. If the payment does not succeed, the
subscription ends.

**When it ends.** New recordings of that server are kept for 1 day. A recording that got its 30
days while the server was subscribed keeps them.

**Refunds.** Payments are generally not refunded. If you cancel, the subscription stays active
until the end of the period you paid for. Nothing here takes away a right the law where you
live gives you. If we do refund a payment, the subscription can end immediately. Recordings
made while it was paid keep their 30 days.

**Who takes the payment.** RevenueCat sells the subscription through RevenueCat Web Billing,
and Stripe processes the card. We do not see or store your card details. The payment form asks
for an email address, which RevenueCat uses for receipts and for the customer portal. See the
[privacy policy](privacy.md).
