# Craig vs pawa

Craig and pawa both record Discord voice channels, but they're built for different jobs. Craig gives you a separate track for each speaker. pawa gives you one MP3.

## Side by side

|                     | Craig                                         | pawa                                 |
|:--------------------|:----------------------------------------------|:-------------------------------------|
| What you get        | A track per speaker: FLAC, AAC, or an Audacity project | One mixed MP3                 |
| Start and finish    | `/join`, then download from a web page        | `/record`, then `/save`              |
| Where the file goes | A download page                               | Posted in your Discord channel       |
| Longest recording   | 6 hours                                       | About 4.5 hours (256 MB)             |
| How long it's kept  | 7 days                                        | 24 hours, or 30 days with Pawa Starter |
| Price               | Free, with extras for supporters              | Free, with one paid plan             |

Craig's numbers come from [craig.chat](https://craig.chat) as of October 2026.

## Where Craig wins

- **A track for each person.** If you edit your recordings, this is the big one. You can fix one person's volume, cut a cough, or clean up a noisy mic without touching anyone else.
- **Years of trust.** Craig has been around a long time and a lot of people run their shows on it.
- **Built for podcasts.** It exports Audacity and Audition projects. Supporters also get cloud uploads and transcripts.

## Where pawa wins

- **One file.** There's nothing to mix. The MP3 plays anywhere: a phone, a browser, Discord itself.
- **Two commands.** `/record` to start, `/save` to finish.
- **No setup.** Invite it and go. There's no dashboard to learn.
- **It ends up in Discord.** The recording gets posted in the channel, so your group can hit play right there.

## Verdict

Use Craig when you need separate tracks. Use pawa when you want one file fast.
